GX27 - Patch v27 (PIC Finance otomatis untuk task Finance & Procurement)

File yang berubah: app.js, styles.css, index.html, needs.js. Tidak perlu SQL.
Patch ini sudah termasuk semua perubahan v9, v9b, v10 - v26.

1. Upload app.js, styles.css, index.html, needs.js ke repo GitHub (timpa yang lama).
2. Commit, tunggu Vercel deploy, lalu Ctrl+Shift+R sekali.

CHANGES v27
1. Semua task di tab "Finance & Procurement" otomatis punya PIC "Finance" (ditambahkan di samping PIC lain, tidak mengganti).
   Berlaku untuk task yang punya item budget ter-link, atau kategori Procurement
   (checklist Need per Station tidak ikut kecuali punya item budget). Task baru otomatis ikut.
   "Finance" otomatis masuk daftar PIC (Manage Categories > PIC); kalau namanya diganti di sana, auto-assign ikut nama baru.
2. Kalau Finance sengaja dihapus dari PIC sebuah task (lewat edit PIC / form task), app mengingatnya dan tidak menambahkannya lagi.
   Tambahkan Finance lagi kapan saja untuk menyalakan kembali.

CHANGES v26 (included)
1. Form item budget: field baru "Funding":
   - New budget (default) -> menambah Total Budget seperti biasa.
   - From Buffer Budget -> TIDAK menambah Total Budget; biayanya diambil dari Buffer Budget.
   Di bawah field tampil "Available buffer: Rp ...". Kalau biaya > buffer yang tersedia, muncul peringatan (tetap bisa disimpan).
   Hanya untuk baris utama (sub item ikut budget induknya).
2. Kartu "Buffer Budget · Available" = penghematan confirmed − over budget − biaya item yang diambil dari buffer.
   Detail: Confirmed Savings, Over Budget, Used from Buffer, Available Buffer + daftar item "Funded from Buffer".
3. Item yang diambil dari buffer ditandai "Funded from Buffer"; biayanya = invoice kalau sudah ada, kalau belum = estimasi.
   Pembayarannya tetap masuk Paid / Outstanding Payment. Kartu Total Budget menulis "+ Rp ... from buffer".
4. Fix: kartu Total Budget tidak lagi menulis "filter on ✕" padahal tidak ada filter.
5. Section di dalam Budget Pool (mis. Emergency Fund): tombol "Sections" di baris Emergency Fund.
   Tambah / ganti nama / urutkan / hapus section sendiri (mis. Reimbursement, Konsumsi, Transport).
   Budget tetap satu di Emergency Fund; sub item dikelompokkan per section dengan "N item · used Rp ...".
   Tombol "+ Item" di tiap section langsung mengisi section-nya; di form item ada pilihan Section.
   Ganti nama section -> sub item ikut. Hapus section -> sub item jadi "No section" (tidak terhapus).

CHANGES v23 (included)
1. Semua baris yang punya sub item (Budget Pool dan Vendor Invoice: Doorprize, OJISAN, Bali Games, BUMI, Emergency Fund, dll.)
   menampilkan di bawah nama item: Budget · Realization · Remaining (atau Over, merah) + bar.
   Budget = estimasi baris induk (atau total estimasi sub item kalau induk kosong).
   Realization = invoice / nominal transfer (Vendor Invoice) atau total harga sub item (Budget Pool).
2. Kolom baru "Buffer Budget" (tampil otomatis; bisa disembunyikan lewat Columns):
   Estimated Budget − realisasi, untuk setiap item yang punya estimasi + invoice/nominal. Hijau = sisa, merah = over.
   "pending" = invoice belum submitted / belum lunas. Sub item ditandai "·" karena sudah dihitung di induknya (tidak dobel).
   Subtotal per kategori dan Grand Total ikut menjumlah Buffer Budget.
   (Kartu "Buffer Budget" di atas tetap hanya menghitung penghematan yang sudah confirmed.)

CHANGES v17 (included)

   Filters: All Tasks / Milestones Only (the previous view), workstream pills (Show All / Hide All),
   Category, Status (Not done yet / Overdue / Done) and Search. "Clear Filters" resets everything.

     10000 -> "Rp 10.000". Values are still saved as plain numbers, so totals and calculations are unchanged.

CHANGES v16 (included)
1. Collapsible sidebar: ☰ button fixed at the top-left shows / hides the sidebar; the page content widens automatically.
   The choice is remembered in each browser. Works on desktop and mobile (on mobile it hides the top menu bar).
2. Presentation dashboard: new menu item "Presentation" (#present), built live from the same Tasks & Finance data
   (no copied or hardcoded numbers):
   - Task Progress: donut + Completed / In Progress / Pending / Blocked / Not Started, and Overdue count.
   - Budget Summary: Total Budget, Paid, Outstanding Payment, Buffer Budget (click for the savings detail) + paid/outstanding bar.
   - Overdue items and Upcoming (next 14 days) — tasks and unpaid payment deadlines.
   - Recent Updates — latest edited tasks and budget items.
   - Progress by Workstream.
   - "Full Screen" button for meetings (hides the sidebar; press Esc to exit). Every item is clickable to open its task / budget item.

CHANGES v15 (included)
1. Overview: all text and labels in English (cards, lists, workstream progress, Needs Verification). Short amounts use M / B (Rp 82.6M).
2. Finance summary cards: Total Budget, Paid, Outstanding Payment, Buffer Budget.
3. Buffer Budget card is clickable -> item-level savings (Original Budget, Actual Cost, Savings), plus Over Budget items,
   Net Buffer, and "potential savings not counted yet".

   - No double counting: line items inside a vendor invoice are compared once at invoice level; Emergency Fund allocation

   Combines with all other filters (category, payment status, invoice status, search). Line items follow their vendor invoice's due date.
